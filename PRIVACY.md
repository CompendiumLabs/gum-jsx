# Gum Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026.

## About this policy

Compendium Labs LLC provides the Gum authoring plugin and renderer. This policy
describes how Compendium Labs handles information in connection with the plugin,
renderer, and support requests.

## Local rendering

Gum renders figures in the environment where you run it. This may be your own
computer or a cloud environment provided by your AI host. Compendium Labs does
not receive your prompts, source files, input data, or rendered output through
the plugin or renderer. Gum does not send usage data or telemetry to Compendium
Labs.

Because Compendium Labs does not collect this rendering data, it does not retain,
sell, or share it. Files created during rendering remain in your execution
environment. You can delete them using that environment's file controls.

## Software downloads and other providers

Initial setup may download the Gum renderer from the npm registry. The registry
provider handles these network requests under its own privacy policy. Rendering
itself runs locally and requires no communication with Compendium Labs.

Your AI host processes conversations and may store files or run commands in its
own cloud environment. Its privacy policy and retention controls apply to that
processing. This policy describes Compendium Labs' practices and does not replace
the policies of your AI host, npm, GitHub, or other services you choose to use.

## Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include.
We use that information to respond to your request. Email service providers
process correspondence to deliver and store it.

We delete support correspondence within 12 months after the request is resolved.
You can request earlier deletion by contacting us at the address below.

If you open a public GitHub issue, your issue and attachments may be visible to
others. Avoid including personal information, credentials, or confidential
files. GitHub's privacy policy and account controls apply to information posted
there.

For privacy questions or requests to delete information you have sent us, contact
[doug@compendiumlabs.ai](mailto:doug@compendiumlabs.ai).

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change.
